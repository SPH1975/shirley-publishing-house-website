        Peer Review Process

        Shirley Publishing House

        Shirley Publishing House upholds a thorough and impartial review system to ensure that every manuscript meets academic and ethical standards. Each submission is examined for originality, scholarly value, clarity, and relevance before any publication decision is made.

        1. After submission, the manuscript is screened for compliance with journal requirements, overall quality, and originality.
        2. Submissions that pass initial screening are endorsed to the Editorial Board for preliminary assessment.
        3. The Editorial Board assigns the manuscript to two independent anonymous reviewers for evaluation.
        4. Reviewers assess the manuscript using the criteria shown below.
        5. A publication decision is usually issued within one to two weeks after the final reviewer reports are received.
        ✓

        Novelty and originality of the study or creative work

        ✓

        Importance and contribution of the problem or work to the field

        ✓

        Soundness of the concept, method, or technical approach

        ✓

        Quality and insightfulness of the analysis and discussion

        ✓

        Overall contribution of the manuscript

        ✓

        Academic writing quality and presentation
