Immediate reader access, clear reuse rights and unrestricted scholarly discovery for National Research Journal.

Reader access

No login, registration, subscription, payment, cookie consent or form completion is required to open or download publicly available articles. Full-text articles are made freely accessible upon publication without an embargo period.

Licence and reuse

Unless otherwise clearly indicated on the individual article, research articles published by National Research Journal are licensed under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International Licence. This licence permits copying, distribution, adaptation and commercial reuse, provided that appropriate credit is given to the author or authors, the original publication is identified, the licence is linked and any changes are indicated.

Copyright

Authors retain copyright in their articles. Publication by the journal does not transfer copyright ownership to Shirley Publishing House.

Self-archiving

Authors may deposit the submitted manuscript, accepted manuscript and published Version of Record in lawful personal, institutional or disciplinary repositories without embargo. Deposited copies should identify the article’s original publication in National Research Journal and link to the journal record when available.

Machine access

Search engines, repositories, indexing services and scholarly databases may crawl, harvest and index publicly available article metadata and full text. Automated access must remain lawful and should respect reasonable technical safeguards that protect the website’s availability.
